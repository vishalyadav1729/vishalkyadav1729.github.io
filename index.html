About

I completed my Ph.D. in pure mathematics at Memorial University of Newfoundland in September 2026. My research focuses on gradings on Lie algebras, algebraic groups and group schemes, and combinatorial game theory. Recent work includes cyclic gradings and misère games.

I built RiskRate, an auto insurance pricing engine that estimates pure premiums from claim frequency and severity. It includes an interactive pricing app.

My hospital readmission project uses synthetic health records to predict 30-day readmissions and compares interpretable and ensemble models.
