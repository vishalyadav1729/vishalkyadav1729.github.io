About

I completed my Ph.D. in pure mathematics at Memorial University of Newfoundland in September 2026. My work focuses on algebra, with particular interest in Lie theory, gradings on Lie algebras, algebraic groups, and combinatorial game theory.

Recent research includes misère games, cyclic group gradings in prime characteristic, and fine gradings on exceptional Lie algebras such as E6. I also teach mathematics and have supported students through lectures, tutorials, and the Math Help Centre.
