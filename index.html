About

I earned a Ph.D. in pure mathematics from Memorial University of Newfoundland in 2026. My doctoral work extends methods for classifying cyclic gradings of classical Lie algebras across characteristics. I have coauthored a peer-reviewed paper on combinatorial game theory and a preprint on Lie algebra gradings, and presented my research at an international workshop.

Using Python and SQL, I built RiskRate, an insurance pricing pipeline using more than 678,000 motor policies. I found and corrected a data issue that overstated estimated pure premiums by 36.3%, and deployed an interactive pricing app.

For an Erdős Institute team hospital readmission project, I helped build models from synthetic health records to predict 30-day readmissions. Our random forest reached about 0.90 AUC and 0.94 recall in patient-level cross-validation. I completed the institute’s Data Science Boot Camp with Distinction.
