01 / About

About me.

I earned a Ph.D. in pure mathematics from Memorial University of Newfoundland in 2026, where I was advised by Mikhail Kotchetov. My doctoral research focused on gradings of classical Lie algebras and how their classification changes across characteristics. I also work in combinatorial game theory, especially misère games.

Alongside my mathematical research, I build data science projects in insurance pricing and predictive modelling. In RiskRate, my independent auto insurance pricing application, I analysed 678,013 policies and corrected a mismatch between reported and paid claims that had overstated the baseline pure-premium estimate by 36.3%. The project combines Python modelling, independent SQL validation and documentation of model assumptions and limitations.

Teaching and mentoring are important parts of my work. I have taught Mathematical Finance at the University of Delhi and mentored a data science team at the Erdős Institute. I also co-developed Math-MATE with the Learning Disabilities Association of Newfoundland and Labrador, helping translate educators’ input into a funded numeracy program for children with varied learning needs.

Data science / Insurance

RiskRate

An insurance pricing pipeline built with Python and SQL using more than 678,000 motor policies. I corrected a data issue that overstated estimated pure premiums by 36.3% and deployed an interactive pricing app.

Data science / Healthcare

Hospital readmission prediction

With an Erdős Institute team, I helped model 30-day readmissions from synthetic health records. Our random forest reached about 0.90 AUC and 0.94 recall in patient-level cross-validation.
