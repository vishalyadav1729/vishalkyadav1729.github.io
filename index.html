Welcome

Hi, I’m Vishal Yadav.

I study algebra, Lie theory, and combinatorial games.

My research looks for patterns in mathematical structures—from gradings on Lie algebras to the behavior of misère games. I’m currently a Ph.D. student at Memorial University of Newfoundland.

Teaching is also an important part of my work. I enjoy making complex ideas more approachable through courses, tutorials, and one-to-one support.
