Data science · Health

Predicting 30-Day Hospital Readmissions

Erdős Institute Data Science Boot Camp · Completed with Distinction

As part of a team capstone, I worked with synthetic health records to compare logistic regression and random forest models for predicting 30-day readmissions. Using patient-level cross-validation to prevent leakage, our random forest reached about 0.90 AUC and 0.94 recall.

Data science · Insurance

RiskRate: Auto Insurance Pricing Engine

I built a frequency–severity pricing pipeline using more than 678,000 motor policies and corrected a data issue that overstated estimated pure premiums by 36.3%. I compared statistical and machine learning models and deployed an interactive pricing app.

Education · Accessibility

Math-MATE: Early Numeracy for K–2 Learners

As a co-developer and Curriculum Developer with the Learning Disabilities Association of Newfoundland and Labrador, I designed curriculum-aligned mathematics activities for young learners with diverse needs. The project brought research-informed teaching and accessibility principles into a technology-supported learning experience, with feedback from user testing to guide the activities.
