Background

About

I completed my Ph.D. in pure mathematics at Memorial University of Newfoundland in September 2026. My work focuses on algebra, especially Lie theory, gradings, and combinatorial game theory.

Research

I study gradings on Lie algebras, algebraic groups, and structures in prime characteristic. Recent work includes cyclic group gradings and fine gradings on exceptional Lie algebras such as E6.

I’m also interested in combinatorial game theory, particularly questions about misère games, invertibility, reductions, and expansions.

Teaching

I have taught undergraduate and graduate mathematics, including real analysis, mathematical finance, and advanced group theory. As a teaching assistant, I have led tutorials, marked assignments, and supported students through the Math Help Centre.

I value clear explanations and the moment an abstract idea begins to feel approachable.

Beyond mathematics

My projects also reach beyond pure mathematics. I have contributed to curriculum development for accessible K–2 education and worked on a data science project predicting 30-day hospital readmissions.
