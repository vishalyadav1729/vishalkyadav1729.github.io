Teaching & outreach

Experience

Teaching mathematics and contributing to accessible learning projects.

Teaching

I have taught undergraduate and graduate mathematics, including real analysis, mathematical finance, and advanced group theory. As a teaching assistant, I have led tutorials, marked assignments, and supported students through the Math Help Centre.

Beyond mathematics

As a Curriculum Developer with the Learning Disabilities Association of Newfoundland and Labrador, I researched ways to teach early mathematics to children with learning disabilities. I helped design accessible K–2 curriculum materials that bring technology into the learning experience and give students different ways to engage with foundational ideas.

I have also worked on a data science project using synthetic health records to predict 30-day hospital readmissions.
